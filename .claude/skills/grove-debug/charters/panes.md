# The pane system: splits, dividers, tabs, placement

Open panes of every type the picker offers, in several arrangements. Split, resize
by dragging dividers, drag one down to nothing and see what happens, close panes,
reopen them. Move between them with the keyboard as well as the mouse.

Watch for: a pane that will not shrink or will not grow, a divider that jumps,
focus landing somewhere invisible, a layout that does not survive opening a diff
or switching worktrees, chrome that overlaps its content, panes that appear
somewhere other than where the picker was.
